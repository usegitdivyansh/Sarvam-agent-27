# SAMPLE POLICY (demo figures only, replace with the real bank policy)

## Common to all products
CM-1: Identity proof (Aadhaar or Voter ID) and address proof are required. PAN is also required for loans above Rs 1,00,000.
CM-2: Applicant age must be 21 to 60 years at the time of application.
CM-3: Final approval is given only by a loan officer after document verification. The phone agent only pre-qualifies.
CM-4: Total monthly EMI obligations including the new loan must not exceed 50% of verified monthly net income.
CM-5: Prepayment is free after 6 EMIs. Before that a charge of 2% of outstanding principal applies.

## Product A: Udyog Micro-Loan (shop owners and small businesses)
UM-1: Business must have been running for at least 2 years.
UM-2: Verified monthly net income of at least Rs 20,000, shown by the last 6 months bank statement or a GST or income proof.
UM-3: Loan amount from Rs 50,000 to Rs 5,00,000.
UM-4: Tenure from 12 to 48 months.
UM-5: Interest 15% to 21% per year on reducing balance. The exact rate is set at approval.
UM-6: Processing fee 2% of the loan amount plus GST.

## Product B: Gig Quick Loan (delivery riders, drivers, platform workers)
GW-1: Applicant must have been active on a platform for at least 6 months.
GW-2: Verified monthly net income of at least Rs 12,000, shown by the platform earnings statement of the last 3 months.
GW-3: Loan amount from Rs 10,000 to Rs 1,00,000.
GW-4: Tenure from 6 to 24 months.
GW-5: Interest 18% to 26% per year on reducing balance. The exact rate is set at approval.
GW-6: Processing fee 1% of the loan amount plus GST.
